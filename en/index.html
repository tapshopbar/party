How it works

From inquiry to the big day, in five steps.

Leave the details to us. All you need to decide is the date and guest count.

01

Send an inquiry

Share your date, guest count and preferred setup in the form below. It only takes 2 minutes.

02

Quote within 24 hours of review

We check the date and space, and email a detailed quote only if we can host you.

03

Approval · Confirmed with deposit

Once TAP SHOP BAR approves your date, please pay the deposit (20% of the total) within 3 days. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

04

Menu & styling

2–4 weeks before the event, we finalize the menu, wines, setup and flow together.

05

The big day

Your dedicated manager is with you from start to finish. The balance is settled on the day.

Cancellation & Refund

Cancellation & refund policy.

Please review before booking. Paying the deposit means you agree to the terms below.

30+ days before the event
100% refund
Your full deposit is returned.
29–14 days before
50% refund
Half of your deposit is returned.
13 days before to event day
Non-refundable
The deposit is not refunded.
  • The deposit is 20% of the total, and your booking is confirmed once payment is received. If the deposit isn't paid within 3 days of approving the quote, the provisional hold is released.
  • One free date change, up to 30 days before the event. Changes after that follow the cancellation policy.
  • Please confirm your final guest count 7 days before the event . If numbers drop after that, we'll bill based on the confirmed count.
  • The balance is settled on the event day , by card or bank transfer. We issue tax invoices and cash receipts.
  • If TAP SHOP BAR is unable to host your event , we'll first offer an equivalent location or date. If that doesn't suit you, we'll refund your deposit in full.
  • In cases of force majeure such as natural disasters, we'll first help you reschedule, and if that isn't possible, we'll give you a full refund.

Cancellation fees never exceed the deposit (20% of the total). Deadlines are counted from the event date, and a cancellation takes effect on the day we receive it by email. We'll send reminder emails 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before the event.
